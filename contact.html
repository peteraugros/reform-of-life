Before you write

Three things this is not

None of these is a brush-off. Each one has somewhere better to go, and saying so is more use to you than a reply from me would be.

It is not urgent help

Nobody watches this inbox. Messages might sit for a week. If you are in danger, or thinking about hurting yourself, please stop reading and call or text 988 (the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, in the United States, free and around the clock). Outside the US, your local emergency number. That is not a lesser option than writing here; it is the right one, and the people answering are trained for exactly this.

It is not spiritual direction

Direction is an ongoing relationship, and it belongs with a priest, who can also hear your confession and is bound by the seal in a way that no layman is. Ask at the parish office; every parish either has someone or knows who to send you to, and it is an ordinary request rather than a strange one. That is a better arrangement than anything I could offer.

It is not the way for my students to reach me

If you are a student of mine, come and find me at school, or go to campus ministry. That is not distance, it is the arrangement that protects you: at school there are people around me who can help properly, and here there are not. Please use the ordinary channels, and do use them.

The form

Write

Questions about anything on this site, corrections, or someone to pray for. All of it is read. Not all of it can be answered.

Leaving the email box blank sends the message with nothing attached to it. If you fill it in, it is used to write back and for nothing else. Either way this is not a mailing list and there is nothing to unsubscribe from.
