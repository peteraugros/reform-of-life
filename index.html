Four practices

Reform of Life

Nobody arrives at any of this knowing how it works. These are the practical instructions, the ones that are usually assumed and rarely said out loud.

  1. I Daily · about 20 minutes The Rosary The beads keep count so your mind is free to do the actual work. All twenty mysteries, every prayer in full, and what to do with the ten beads in between.
  2. II Daily · about 10 minutes The Gospels Four accounts of the same life, each written for different people. Where to start, how to read slowly enough for it to land, and a first month that ends with a whole Gospel behind you.
  3. III Every month or so Confession Almost everyone who stays away stays away because they do not know what to say. Here is exactly what you say, in order, including the part where you admit you have forgotten how.
  4. IV Every Sunday · about an hour The Mass The shape of it, when to stand and kneel and why, the responses you will need, and what happens at communion. Written for someone who does not want to be the one guessing.

Where to begin

Do not begin with all four at once. Pick the one you are closest to already and do that badly for a month; a practice you actually keep beats a better one you abandon in a week.

The order above is roughly the order of difficulty, not the order of importance. If you have been away a long time, the third one is the door back in, and it is a much shorter conversation than you are imagining.
